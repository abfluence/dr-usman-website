About the Surgeon

Precision in every
incision.

Dr. Usman Ali Liaqat is a Board-Certified Plastic & Reconstructive Surgeon practicing at Dr. Khalid Alruhaimi Hospital in Dammam and Dr. Khalid Alruhaimi Complexes in Al Khobar and Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. With over 9 years of expertise and 6,000+ successful procedures, he combines global surgical standards with a deeply personal approach to patient care.

His philosophy is simple: every patient deserves precision, dignity, and results that feel natural — not performed. From transformative body surgeries to delicate facial refinements, every procedure is approached as a work of art.
